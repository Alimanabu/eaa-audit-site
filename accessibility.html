What this covers

This statement covers the seven pages of eaa-audit.net: the English home page (/), the French home page (/fr/), the sample report page (/sample.html) and its French version (/fr/exemple.html), the terms of service, the privacy policy and this statement. It also covers the sample report in PDF. The address /pricing.html only redirects to the pricing section of the home page.

Our aim and our status

We aim for WCAG 2.1 level AA. We have not done a full evaluation, so we do not claim that this website conforms.

How we tested

On 12 November 2026 we scanned the seven pages with our own tool: axe-core 4.14.0 rules for WCAG 2.1 A and AA in desktop view at 1366 by 900 pixels. It found 0 failures on all seven pages. Automated rules cover only part of WCAG, so this does not show conformance. On the same day we checked that none of the seven pages scrolls sideways at widths of 320, 390, 768, 1280 and 1366 pixels.

A scripted keyboard pass in the same desktop view reached 22 focus stops on the English home page, 22 on the French home page, 13 on each of the two sample report pages and 1 on each of the other three pages, with a visible focus outline on each stop. On the four pages that have a skip link, the skip link moved focus into the main content. No keyboard trap was found. On narrow screens the home pages show fewer menu links, so there are fewer stops.

We have not tested this website with a screen reader, on a phone, or with people who have disabilities. We have not tested it against WCAG 2.2.

Known limitations

The sample report PDF is not tagged, so a screen reader cannot follow its headings and tables. Write to us and we will send the content in a form you can read. The terms of service, the privacy policy and this statement are in English only; the French pages link to them and say so.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this website does not work for you, write to hello@eaa-audit.net. We aim to reply within one business day. You may also contact the authority responsible for accessibility in your country.
