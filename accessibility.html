What this covers

This statement covers the four pages of eaa-audit.net: the home page, the terms of service, the privacy policy and this statement. It also covers the sample report in PDF.

Our aim and our status

We aim for WCAG 2.1 level AA. We have not done a full evaluation, so we do not claim that this website conforms.

How we tested

On 31 October 2026 we scanned the four pages with our own tool: axe-core 4.14.0 rules for WCAG 2.1 A and AA in desktop view. It found 0 failures on all four pages. Automated rules cover only part of WCAG, so this does not show conformance. A scripted keyboard pass on the home page reached 17 focus stops with a visible focus change on each, the skip link worked, and no keyboard trap was found.

We have not tested this website with a screen reader, on a phone, or with people who have disabilities. We have not tested it against WCAG 2.2.

Known limitations

The sample report PDF is not tagged, so a screen reader cannot follow its headings and tables. Write to us and we will send the content in a form you can read.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this website does not work for you, write to hello@eaa-audit.net. We aim to reply within one business day. You may also contact the authority responsible for accessibility in your country.
